Product Overview

What is Annapurna Tiffin?

Annapurna Tiffin is an end-to-end management, dispatch, and pro-rated billing operating system built specifically for home-style lunch delivery services. Unlike generic SaaS recurring subscription platforms, Annapurna is designed around weekday-only fresh meal logistics. It tracks flexible customer pauses (for festivals, business trips, vacations, or sick days) and calculates fair, mathematical month-end bills where every customer is charged only for the lunches cooked and delivered to them.

Core Capabilities

Built for Real-World Tiffin Logistics

Calendar Weekday Pro-Rating

Dynamically computes daily rates from the actual working weekdays in the month (20 to 23 days). Zero rounding drift — full attendance always matches the exact plan price.

Dynamic Pause & Resume

Schedule fixed pause ranges or open-ended indefinite pauses. Overlapping pause protection prevents duplicate records and accidental charges.

Morning Kitchen Dispatch

Instant 6:00 AM visibility into Active (to cook & pack) vs. Paused (do not cook) customers, eliminating kitchen waste and forgotten meals.

Instant Phone Lookup

Search by customer phone number to instantly retrieve their current status, active pause window, pause history, and live month-to-date estimated bill.

Itemized Day Audit Invoices

Generate clear, 1-click printable tax invoices with a day-by-day delivery calendar breakdown so customers can verify every single meal.

Search, Sort & Paginate

Comprehensive customer search across names, phone numbers, and addresses with dynamic sorting (ASC/DESC) and responsive pagination across web & REST APIs.

Who It Is For

Target Audience

  • Independent Home Chefs & Tiffin Centers: Serving 20 to 500 daily office lunches who currently rely on paper notebooks or WhatsApp chats.
  • Dabbawala Collectives: Coordinating regional multi-carrier delivery networks with daily active/paused route changes.
  • Corporate Lunch Caterers: Managing corporate employee meal subsidies where HR requires attendance-based monthly billing.
Tangible Impact

How It Helps

  • Zero Food Waste: The kitchen only buys groceries and cooks meals for customers verified active for today.
  • 15+ Hours Saved Monthly: Eliminates painful month-end manual math and tallying WhatsApp chat timestamps.
  • 100% Trust & Zero Disputes: Customers receive itemized calendar audit receipts showing exact dates delivered vs. paused.
